# kan-chan

A one-page website to learn Japanese kana (hiragana, katakana) and JLPT kanji, with kan-chan, a little manga mascot.

- **Kana**: sound and stroke-order chart, "find them all" writing game, practice sheets, flashcards, top words, phrases and a translator.
- **Kanji**: JLPT N5 to N1 levels, stroke animations, readings, tracing, practice sheets, flashcards and words per level.

Everything is in [`index.html`](index.html). There is no server and no database: it runs on GitHub Pages as a plain web page.

## Your progress

Progress is saved **in your browser, on your device** (browser storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
To move it to another device or keep a backup, open **💾 My progress**, press **Download my progress**, then on the other device press **Load a progress file**.
